# Data dictionary

Fields of the versioned hydrology files. The raw NetCDFs and the optional
human-readable reports are regenerated by the scripts and are not versioned.

## data/derived/annual_discharge.csv
One row per country-year (selected river cell).
- country: Ethiopia or Zimbabwe
- site: raw subfolder name
- year: calendar year
- valid_daily_count: number of valid daily records (>=350 to be used)
- mean_daily_discharge_m3s: calendar-year mean discharge at the selected cell
- normalized_annual_flow_raw: mean_daily_discharge_m3s / period mean
- hydrology_state: L, N, or H (empirical tercile of the annual series)

## data/derived/annual_hydrology_states.csv
Compact country, site, year, hydrology_state (subset of annual_discharge.csv).

## data/derived/transition_counts.csv
Raw observed year-to-year transitions.
- country, origin_state, destination_state, count (integer N_ij)

## data/derived/transition_probabilities.csv
- country, origin_state, destination_state
- count: N_ij
- row_total: N_i (sum over destinations)
- p_empirical: N_ij / row_total  (BASELINE transition probability)
- p_jeffreys_alpha_0_5: (N_ij + 0.5) / (row_total + 1.5)  (SENSITIVITY ONLY)

## outputs/hydro_calibration.json  (machine-readable contract, schema gep-hydro-calibration/1.0)
Per country:
- calibration_period, n_complete_years, dropped_partial_years
- selected_gauge (lat, lon, catchment_km2, catchment_src)
- selected_grid_cell (lat, lon, mean_m3s, dist_deg, n_channel_cells)
- mean_annual_m3s, terciles_m3s, n_years_LNH
- mu_raw, stationary_weighted_mu_raw, mu_normalized
- transition_counts (N_ij), P_H_empirical (BASELINE), stationary_distribution
- h1, hydro_capacity_factor, max_fhy_times_mu
- validation: mu_monotone, rows_sum_to_1, counts_reconcile,
  pi_stationary_maxdev, sum_pi_mu_norm, f_hy_mu_max, f_hy_mu_le_1
- diagnostics.transition_sensitivity.jeffreys_alpha_0_5 (NON-baseline)

The model input builder `model/export_params.py` reads `mu_normalized`,
`transition_counts` (row-normalized to `P^H`), `stationary_distribution`, and
`h1` from this file.
